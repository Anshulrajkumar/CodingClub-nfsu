Team Formation Rules

Please read and adhere strictly to the following criteria when building your team:

Exactly 6 members per team, including the Team Leader. 
* Every team MUST have at least 1 female member. 
* All 6 members must belong to the same college/institute (NFSU TC). Inter-college teams are strictly NOT permitted.
* Cross-Branch & Cross-Department Allowed: Members can be from any academic branch or department (e.g., B.Tech - M.Tech CSE (Cyber Security),  B.Sc or M.Sc Forensic Science etc. within a team is allowed and encouraged to bring diverse skill sets).
* Cross-Semester & Cross-Year Allowed: Members can belong to different academic years or semesters (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th or 5th-year students can form a single team together).
