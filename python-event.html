Structured 2-Month Course

Course Overview

The Coding Club successfully organized a comprehensive two-month structured course on Basic Python Programming. This foundational program was designed to introduce students to the world of programming through Python, one of the most beginner-friendly and widely-used programming languages in the industry.

Event Details

On the inaugural day, a total of 33 students attended the session, where the fundamentals of Python were introduced and explained comprehensively by the Vice-President of the Coding Club. The course was structured to provide a solid foundation in Python programming, making it accessible to students of all backgrounds and experience levels.

This initiative by the Coding Club demonstrates the commitment to nurturing technical talent and fostering a culture of continuous learning among the student community. The Python course served as an excellent stepping stone for students interested in pursuing programming and software development as a career path.
