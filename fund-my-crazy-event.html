Fund My Crazy

4 September, 2026

Ideation & Innovation Event

Event Overview

On 4th September 2026, the Coding Club, NFSU Tripura Campus, in collaboration with Code Sage and Google Student Ambassadors — Priyanshu Sahoo and Prasunma Das, successfully organized Fund My Crazy, an exciting ideation and innovation-focused event.

The event witnessed an enthusiastic participation of 70 students, who came together to pitch ideas, present innovative solutions, and engage with mentors on campus.

About This Event

Fund My Crazy gave students a stage to share bold ideas and turn them into structured pitches. With 70 participants, the session mixed presentations, discussion, and recognition, keeping the focus on creativity and practical innovation.

The Coding Club thanks Code Sage and Google Student Ambassadors Priyanshu Sahoo and Prasunma Das for partnering on this event, and every student who showed up to pitch, listen, and learn.
