Team Tech Naari — 1st Place

Debasmita Karmakar, KVN Srinitya, Tanmayee Anasingaraju, Disha Chowdhary • B.Tech–M.Tech Semester 4

Project: Campus Whispers

Campus Whispers is a secure role-based campus management and student support portal developed specifically for National Forensic Sciences University, Tripura Campus. The platform operates through a whitelist-based login system, allowing access only to approved email IDs via Google Sign-In authentication, ensuring a controlled and secure environment.

Team The Trio — 3rd Place

Pratyay Mukherji, Saniya Khatri, Mohit Yadav • B.Sc–M.Sc Semester 6

Project: Automated Campus Printing System (SPARK)

Self-service Print Automation and Record-Keeping (SPARK) is an open-source campus printing system that eliminates manual requests and disorganized queues. Students upload a document, complete a UPI payment, and receive a token — jobs are then auto-processed in order and sent directly to the printer. Admins monitor all activity in real time via a centralized dashboard, keeping campus printing transparent and efficient.
